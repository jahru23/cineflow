# CineFlow — Brand & Logo Guidelines

## 1. The Logo
* **Concept:** *The Fluid C* — An unbroken ribbon forming the letterform "C" that frames a forward Play core. Represents the seamless union of Cinema (*playback action*) and Flow (*continuous, zero-buffering streaming*).
* **Versions:**
  * **Primary Symbol:** Standalone mark for app icons, avatars, and favicons.
  * **Horizontal Lockup:** Primary format for website headers, documentation, and wide banners.
  * **Stacked Lockup:** Centered format for splash screens, posters, cards, and cards.
  * **Small-Size Cut:** Calibrated geometry for micro-scale displays (16px–32px).

---

## 2. Clear Space
Maintain a minimum exclusion zone of **1X** on all sides of the mark, where **X** equals the height of the inner Play triangle (approx. 25% of the symbol's height). No text, buttons, or graphics may enter this boundary.

---

## 3. Minimum Display Sizes
| Asset | Minimum Screen Size | Minimum Print Size | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Horizontal Lockup** | 120 px wide | 32 mm | Below 120 px, switch to standalone symbol |
| **Stacked Lockup** | 100 px wide | 28 mm | Ideal for splash & card interfaces |
| **Symbol Master** | 48 px | 12 mm | Standard UI buttons and navigation |
| **Small-Size Symbol** | 16 px | 5 mm | Use `cineflow-symbol-favicon` / `small` |

### Approved Combinations
* **Dark Mode (Default):** Crimson Symbol + Pure White wordmark on `#0D0D11` / `#16161B`.
* **Light Mode:** Crimson Symbol + Deep `#111111` wordmark on Pure White.
* **Monochrome Inverted:** 100% White mark on dark photos or brand crimson tiles.
* **Monochrome Black:** 100% Black mark for single-ink printing and paper documents.

---

## 5. Typography
* **Wordmark Construction:** Custom geometric sans based on *Liberation Sans / Inter*.
  * **Cine:** Bold / Heavy (800)
  * **Flow:** Regular / Book (400)
* **Application UI Font:** Inter / Roboto / System San-Serif.

---

## 6. Prohibited Usage (Don'ts)
* ❌ Do not stretch, compress, or shear the logo.
* ❌ Do not add artificial drop shadows, glows, strokes, or 3D bevels.
* ❌ Do not alter the relative scale between the symbol and the wordmark.
* ❌ Do not substitute the brand crimson with unauthorized shades of pink or orange.
* ❌ Do not enclose the symbol inside busy, low-contrast photographic backgrounds without a solid card backdrop.
