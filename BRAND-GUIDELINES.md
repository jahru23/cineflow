# CineFlow — Monochrome Brand & Logo Guidelines

## 1. The Logo Concept
* **The Fluid C (Monochrome Edition):** A continuous, sweeping ribbon forming the letterform "C" that frames a central forward Play action core.
* **Aesthetic:** Ultra-clean, high-contrast monochrome design (Pure Black `#000000`, Pure White `#FFFFFF`, and neutral Muted Gray `#888888`), tuned specifically for modern dark-mode developer tools and AMOLED interfaces.

---

## 2. Core Palette
| Tone | HEX | RGB | Application |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **AMOLED Black** | `#000000` | `0, 0, 0` | Primary app background, container cards, icon tiles |
| **Pure White** | `#FFFFFF` | `255, 255, 255` | The Fluid C ribbon, Play core, primary "Cine" wordmark |
| **Muted Gray** | `#888888` | `136, 136, 136` | Secondary "Flow" wordmark, subtle borders and secondary text |

---

## 3. Approved Logo Formats
* **Dark Mode (Default):** Pure White symbol + White/Gray wordmark on `#000000`.
* **Light Mode:** Pure Black symbol + Black/Gray wordmark on `#FFFFFF`.
* **Themed Icons (Android 13+):** 100% vector monochrome mask dynamically tinted by Android Material You Monet engine.
* **App Icon:** White symbol centered on pure black squircle tile.

---

## 4. Minimum Sizes & Clear Space
* **Clear Space:** Maintain **1X** padding on all sides (where X = height of the play core).
* **Minimum Screen Width:** 120 px for horizontal lockup; 16 px for standalone symbol (`cineflow-symbol-small`).

---

## 5. Prohibited Usage (Don'ts)
* ❌ Do not stretch, compress, or shear the logo.
* ❌ Do not add artificial drop shadows, glows, strokes, or 3D bevels.
* ❌ Do not alter the relative scale between the symbol and the wordmark.
* ❌ Do not enclose the symbol inside busy backgrounds without a solid card backdrop.
